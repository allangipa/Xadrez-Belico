Method

What was in plain sight, read again

Military history readers correct dates, units and calibers — and rightly so. Every number here carries a seal saying how far it can be trusted.

When the sources disagree, we show the versions and do not pick one. Unit names and times only go in after being verified. And no image on this site was generated by AI: it is period material, a military map or a photo of the place, with the year on screen.

  • 2+ sources

    ConfirmedConfirmed in two or more independent sources.

  • 1 source

    Single sourceA single source; the text says which.

  • disputed

    DisagreementThe sources disagree; we show the versions and do not pick one.

  • no record

    No recordNobody recorded it. We say so instead of making it up.
